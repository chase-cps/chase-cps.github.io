Demos#

The demos are in the demos repository; Running the demos says how to run them. They are grouped by the kind of system. The tags after each demo say what it uses.

AI agents#

The plan of an AI agent, as a contract over its tool calls.

  • Payment assistant agent: the plan of an AI agent, split among three sub-agents, checked before it runs; the quotient says which sub-agent must send the text. algebra verification agents

Aircraft and vehicles#

Requirements of aircraft and cars, on Booleans and real numbers.

  • NASA Lift-Plus-Cruise aircraft: the requirements of a NASA aircraft are consistent with a hover in 12 steps, not in 6; the quotient says what the pilot must do. algebra verification reals
  • Lockheed Martin autopilot: the unrealizable cores find the requirements in conflict, fixed one at a time. verification GR(1)
  • Aircraft power system: requirements written as patterns: the bounds as written conflict, and the core points to one DC bus; relaxed, they are realizable. DSL GR(1)

Hardware#

Contracts in SystemVerilog Assertions, checked against RTL, and synthesized arbiters.

  • OpenTitan security chip: the escalation link proven module by module from its RTL; a faster sender breaks its contract. algebra verification SVA RTL
  • Circuit buffer swap: a smaller buffer fails the old contract, but refines the quotient: the contract of its slot. algebra verification SVA RTL
  • AMBA AHB bus arbiter: two viewpoints that cannot be merged, the assumption that fixes them, and the arbiter synthesized. algebra verification GR(1) SVA
  • Resource arbiter: the least bound on the waiting time of a request, repaired from the counterexamples of nuXmv; the arbiter and its client as state machines in SMV, checked for implementation, receptiveness, and environment. verification repair SMV

Software#

C functions checked one at a time against contracts written in the C file.

  • Contracts in C: functions of a water tank, a ring buffer, and a drone, each checked against the contract written in its C file (ESBMC, CBMC, or ACSL clauses); code with no contract checked for overflow. verification C
  • Fire-suppression drones in C: two drones on a grid; a collision found on the contracts, the rule of the coordinator fixed with the quotient, then each C function checked alone. algebra verification C
  • Room thermostat: a thermostat overshoots by 2 degrees; repair finds the threshold, ESBMC checks that its C code implements it, and the check between the samples says which threshold holds in continuous time. verification signals robustness continuous time repair C

Drones and robots#

Drones, robots, and their controllers, checked and synthesized.

  • Drone climb: a drone climbs in the wind of a fire; no climb rate is enough, so repair says what the requirement can ask, and a cost chooses between two constants. verification signals robustness repair
  • Robot charging station: five arbiters for a station shared by two robots; refinement does not see the conflict, realizability does, and each flaw is caught by another check. algebra verification GR(1)
  • Robotic arm joints: three joints refine the contract of the arm and are synthesized one at a time; the check scales to 8 joints in 5 s. algebra verification GR(1)
  • Pick-and-place arm: an arm that picks and places a piece; the mission planner refines the requirement, the seven contracts fit together, and the coordinator is synthesized. verification GR(1)
  • Drone timing: the timing of fire-suppression drones, reaction by reaction: the bound of 400 ms holds with 120 ms to spare, a timer of 250 ms fails, and repair finds the least change. verification reals repair
