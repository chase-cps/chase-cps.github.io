# About

CHASE started at the University of Southern California and the University
of Verona, and is developed at the University of California, Berkeley. It is
open source, under the BSD 3-Clause License.

- [Publications](publications.md): the papers on CHASE and how to cite it.
- [Repositories](repositories.md): where each part of CHASE lives on GitHub.
- [Contributing](contributing.md): reporting bugs, building and testing, and
  proposing changes.
